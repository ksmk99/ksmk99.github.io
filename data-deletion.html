User Data Deletion Instructions

Word Pile does not have user accounts, and game progress is stored only on your device.

Delete data on your device

Uninstall the Game, or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → Word Pile → Storage → Clear data.

Delete data held by analytics and advertising services

  1. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  2. Send an email to kosarev.me@mail.ru with the subject "Data deletion request" and, if possible, your advertising ID. We will forward the request to our analytics and advertising partners and confirm within 30 days.
