Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy describes how the mobile game Word Pile ("the Game"), published by Maxim Kosarev ("we"), handles information. The Game does not require an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address or any other personal details.

Information collected automatically

When you play, the Game and the third-party services it uses may collect: your device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), device model, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, app version, and gameplay and usage events (for example, levels played and session length).

How we use it

  • to operate and improve the Game and fix errors;
  • to measure how the Game is used and how our marketing performs;
  • to show advertising, including personalised advertising where permitted.

Third-party services

The Game integrates services that process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Voodoo / TinySauce SDK and its advertising partners
  • GameAnalytics (analytics)
  • Adjust (attribution and marketing measurement)
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK (app events and ad measurement)
  • Google Play services

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where required by law, the Game asks for your consent before personalised advertising is shown.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game. To request deletion of data we hold about you, see the data deletion instructions.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
